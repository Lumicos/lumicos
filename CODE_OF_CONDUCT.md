# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different languages, hardware budgets and experience levels. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, unsolicited sexual content and deliberate sharing of another person's private data are not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to provide free support or run unsafe workloads. Ask before sharing someone's screenshots, identity or conversations.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, restrict participation or close discussions. Responses should consider severity and context, explain decisions when safe, and protect reporters' privacy. Good-faith disagreement and respectful correction are welcome.

No private conduct-reporting address has been verified for this project. For sensitive reports, request a private contact channel without posting names, allegations or evidence publicly. Maintainers should publish an appropriate contact before broad community launch. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not use public issues to disclose them. If a private channel cannot be established, do not post sensitive material here.
